Is flat revenue hiding a retention problem?

Online Retail II: a UK gift and homeware wholesaler, 1 Dec 2009 to 9 Dec 2011, 1.07M transaction lines cleaned with Python (pandas). All figures are revenue in GBP, not profit (no cost data). Customer views use the 77% of lines that carry a customer ID. Dec 2011 is partial (data ends 9 Dec).

Data: Chen, D. (2012). Online Retail II [dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository, archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii, licensed CC BY 4.0. This dashboard shows aggregates only; no row-level data is published. Cleaning and analysis code: src/analysis.py.
